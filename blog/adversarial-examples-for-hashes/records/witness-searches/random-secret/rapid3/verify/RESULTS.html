Reading

Both rates are reproduced from the unmodified upstream source with a fresh sampler and two different RNGs. The mechanism is secret-independent (the colliding fold sees uniform operands whichever way the secret is drawn), so the random-secret score equals the default-secret score: 28.5 bits displayed for either witness pair.
