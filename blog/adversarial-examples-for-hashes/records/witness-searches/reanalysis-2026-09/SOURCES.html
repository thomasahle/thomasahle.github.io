Upstream sources used by the programs in these folders

The mc_*.cpp, *_long.cpp and kf_abseil.cpp programs are single files that already contain the SMHasher3 platform header and the verbatim SMHasher3 hashes/*.cpp source of the hash they test, plus a driver; they need nothing else.
