MuseAir v0.3: an 18-byte every-seed family

On the 17..32-byte path the state before finalization is (i, j) = read16(bytes 0..15) ^ T(tail) ^ S(seed, len), where read16 is a bijection of the first 16 bytes (i = lu32(0)<<32 | lu32(12), j = lu32(4)<<32 | lu32(8)) and T is the public product term of the tail. For every 2-byte tail t, the head read16^-1(I ^ T(t)) gives the same state: 2^16 members at 18 bytes (256 at 17 bytes), colliding in all four SMHasher3 variants (64/128-bit, standard/bfast) for every seed.

Build and run:

c++ -O2 -std=c++17 mc_museair.cpp -o mc_museair && ./mc_museair 16
# independent check (fetch a5hash.h, MurmurHash3.cpp, MurmurHash3.h; see ../SOURCES.md)
c++ -O2 -std=c++17 -I. -Ismh3shim indep_museair_a5_murmur.cc MurmurHash3.cpp -o indep_museair_a5_murmur
./indep_museair_a5_murmur 12

The same program also checks the a5hash-128 and MurmurHash3 families (see ../a5hash-128/, ../murmurhash3/).
