Head of Machine Learning, Normal Computing · Copenhagen

My work combines AI and theory. On the AI side, I build agents that design, formalize and verify chips. On the theory side, I work on hashing with machine-checked guarantees, fast polynomial evaluation, optimization theory, and the foundations of massive data: similarity search, sketching and high-dimensional geometry.
